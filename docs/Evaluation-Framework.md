# AI Evaluation Framework

## Goal

Provide transparent, repeatable and explainable interview evaluation.

---

# Scoring Dimensions

| Dimension | Weight |
|-----------|--------|
| Relevance | 25% |
| Structure | 20% |
| Specificity | 20% |
| Business Impact | 20% |
| Clarity | 15% |

---

# Rubric

## Relevance

Questions

- Did the answer address the problem?
- Was anything important ignored?

---

## Structure

Behavioral

- STAR

Product

- Framework driven

---

## Specificity

High Score

- Metrics
- Numbers
- Examples

Low Score

- Generic statements

---

## Business Impact

Evaluation considers

- Revenue
- Growth
- Retention
- Cost
- Customer Experience

---

## Clarity

Measures

- Flow
- Conciseness
- Readability

---

# AI Confidence

Every evaluation should include:

High

Medium

Low

If confidence is Low:

Display

"This evaluation may be less reliable."

---

# Failure Modes

## Hallucination

AI invents candidate achievements.

Mitigation

Resume grounding.

---

## False Praise

Weak answer receives high score.

Mitigation

Human evaluation sampling.

---

## Overly Harsh Feedback

Strong answer receives low score.

Mitigation

Calibration dataset.

---

# Human Review

Users may mark feedback as:

- Helpful
- Not Helpful
- Incorrect

This data should be retained for future model evaluation.
