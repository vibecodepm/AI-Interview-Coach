# Market Research

## Market Opportunity

Demand for Product Management interview preparation continues to grow due to increased competition for PM roles and the rise of AI-assisted hiring.

Candidates increasingly seek tools that provide personalized coaching instead of static content.

---

# Target Audience

Primary

- PM candidates preparing for interviews

Secondary

- MBA students
- Career switchers
- Internal promotion candidates

Future

- Engineering Managers
- Designers
- Data Scientists
- Engineering candidates

---

# Existing Solutions

| Product | Strength | Limitation |
|----------|-----------|------------|
| Interviewing.io | Human interviews | Limited availability |
| Exponent | PM content | Static preparation |
| Pramp | Peer interviews | Feedback quality varies |
| ChatGPT | Flexible | No structured evaluation |
| Claude | Good reasoning | No interview workflow |

---

# Differentiation

AI Interview Coach combines:

- Resume awareness
- JD awareness
- Structured evaluation
- AI coaching
- Progress tracking

Instead of being just another chatbot, the product is designed as a complete interview preparation system.

---

# Risks

- Hallucinated feedback
- Resume parsing failures
- Poor evaluation consistency
- User trust in AI scoring

---

# Mitigations

- Human review capability
- Structured scoring rubric
- Confidence indicators
- Manual resume input fallback

---

# Future Expansion

The platform can expand into:

- Resume optimization
- Career coaching
- Salary negotiation
- Leadership coaching
- Company-specific interview preparation
