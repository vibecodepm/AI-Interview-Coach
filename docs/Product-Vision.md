# Product Vision

## Vision Statement

Empower Product Managers to interview with confidence through personalized, AI-powered coaching that delivers objective, measurable, and actionable feedback.

---

# Mission

Enable every PM candidate to improve interview performance through continuous practice rather than guesswork.

---

# Product Principles

## Personalization

Every interview should adapt to:

- Resume
- Job Description
- Target Role
- Seniority

---

## Explainability

Every AI score should include an explanation.

Users should always understand:

- Why they received the score
- What they did well
- What they should improve

---

## Measurable Improvement

Candidates should see progress over time instead of isolated interview sessions.

---

## Trust

The system must never fabricate achievements or experiences that are not present in the user's resume.

---

## Simplicity

Users should be able to begin a mock interview within two minutes.

---

# North Star Metric

Average Interview Score Improvement after three completed practice sessions.

---

# Product Goals

### Short Term

- Launch MVP
- Validate interview quality
- Collect user feedback

### Medium Term

- AI answer evaluation
- Voice interviews
- Resume parsing

### Long Term

- AI career coach
- Company-specific interview packs
- Personalized learning plans
