# Executive Summary

## Product

AI Interview Coach is an AI-powered interview preparation platform built specifically for Product Management professionals. The platform generates personalized mock interviews using a candidate's resume, target job description, and desired role. It evaluates interview responses using a structured scoring framework and provides actionable feedback to help candidates improve over multiple practice sessions.

---

# Problem

Product Management candidates often struggle to prepare effectively because interview practice is subjective, inconsistent, and difficult to measure.

Current preparation methods include:

- Reading interview books
- Watching YouTube videos
- Practicing with friends
- Memorizing frameworks

These methods rarely provide objective, personalized feedback.

---

# Solution

AI Interview Coach provides:

- Resume-aware interview generation
- JD-specific interview questions
- Structured AI evaluation
- Personalized coaching
- Performance tracking
- Repeatable interview practice
