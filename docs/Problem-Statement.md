# Problem Statement

## Background

Product Management interviews are among the most difficult interviews to prepare for because they evaluate structured thinking, communication, product intuition, leadership, and business understanding simultaneously.

Most candidates receive little or no objective feedback during preparation.

---

# Existing Alternatives

Current preparation methods include:

- Mock interviews with friends
- Interview books
- YouTube content
- Online communities
- Paid coaching

---

# Key Problems

## Problem 1

Feedback is subjective.

Different interviewers provide different opinions for the same answer.

---

## Problem 2

Preparation is generic.

Candidates practice common interview questions instead of questions tailored to their own experience and target company.

---

## Problem 3

Progress is invisible.

Candidates cannot measure whether they are improving over time.

---

## Problem 4

Follow-up questions are unrealistic.

Static question banks cannot adapt based on previous answers.

---

# Opportunity

Large Language Models enable personalized interview generation and structured evaluation at scale.

By combining resume context, job descriptions, and evaluation rubrics, AI can provide more relevant and repeatable interview practice.

---

# Success Criteria

A successful solution should:

- Personalize interview questions
- Evaluate answers consistently
- Provide actionable coaching
- Track improvement over time
